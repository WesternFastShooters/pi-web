# Codex Desktop Behaviors

- Toolbar actions are icon-first, borderless, and gain a soft neutral hover surface.
- Selected sidebar rows use a neutral rounded surface without a colored leading rule.
- Main and sidebar surfaces are separated by a hairline/elevation rather than a heavy border.
- Composer floats over the thread footer with a translucent surface, prominent soft elevation, and 20px radius.
- Detail panels preserve their mounted state while opening and closing.
- Resize handles remain visually quiet until hover or drag.
- Reduced-motion users receive state changes without decorative animation.
- Light and dark modes share geometry and differ only in semantic color tokens.
